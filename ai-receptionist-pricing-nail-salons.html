Pricing questions

How much does an AI receptionist cost for a nail salon?

A website chat assistant is $97 a month. Phone receptionists start at $197 a month, with plans at $197, $297 and $497. Phone plus chat starts at $594 a month.

What is the difference between the $197, $297 and $497 phone plans?

$197 answers when you are closed. $297 answers when your front desk misses a call. $497 answers every call, 24/7.

Is there a free trial?

As a launch offer, the first 20 clients start any plan with a free 7-day trial. Once those spots are taken, the free trial ends.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

There is no long-term contract. Plans are month to month. After the trial there is a one-time setup fee equal to your first month if you keep the service.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Will callers know it is an AI?

Yes. It says it is an AI if asked, and can say so up front if you prefer.
