Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Blue Bot Prints ("we", "us") builds AI voice agents for local businesses and is based in Las Vegas, Nevada. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you fill out a form on our website, book a call, or contact us, we collect the details you give us: your name, business name, email address and mobile phone number, plus any message you send. We may also collect basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited.

How we use your information

We use it to respond to your request, schedule calls and demos, provide and improve our services, and send you information about Blue Bot Prints. If you agree to receive text messages, we use your mobile number to send them as described below.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you check the consent box on our form, you agree to receive text messages from Blue Bot Prints about our services, demos, appointments and follow-ups. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or text messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Information is shared only with service providers who help us deliver our messages and services, and they may use it only for that purpose.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that operate our website, email, phone and messaging tools on our behalf, or when the law requires it.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and out of emails using the unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
