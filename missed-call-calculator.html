About this calculator

How is the number calculated?

Calls you miss per open day, times days open per month, times the share of those callers who would have booked, times your average sale. It uses only the numbers you type.

Is this a promise of results?

No. It is an estimate based on your own guesses. Your real numbers will differ.

How many missed calls are normal for a salon?

It varies a lot by salon. Count for a week, using your voicemail and missed-call log, and use that number.

Does this store my numbers?

No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
