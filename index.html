Questions people ask

Will callers know it's an AI?

Your agent sounds natural, and it is honest if someone asks whether they're talking to an AI. We can also have it say so up front, whichever you prefer.

What happens if the agent can't answer something?

It takes a message or transfers the call to you or your team, with the caller's details already collected.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. We connect the agent to your existing line.

How long does setup take?

Most voice agents are live within about a week of our first call, depending on how many tools we connect.

Do you work with my calendar and software?

We connect to the calendars and tools most businesses already use. Tell us what you run and we'll confirm.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel anytime.
